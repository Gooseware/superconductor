Google is currently aiming to release Gemini 4 "much earlier" than the end of 2026. Fresh confirmation from Google DeepMind's new chief, Koray Kavukcuoglu, reveals that the core pre-training run is complete and the model has officially entered its "post-training" refinement and safety-tuning phase. Given this progress, tech observers speculate that we could see an early iteration or developer preview arrive as soon as October or November 2026. ➡️ How It Will Roll OutWhile Google has not published an exact roadmap, its business strategy is highly expected to mirror its previous major model generations: Developer & Research Previews: Google typically drops early "post-training" outputs to developers via Google AI Studio and Vertex AI. This allows them to gather rapid feedback and test the model's behavior. LMSYS Chatbot Arena Testing: Google frequently soft-launches unreleased variants under "experimental" tags in public benchmarking arenas like LMSYS to see how they stack up against rivals. Phased Tiers: Expect a staggered release starting with lighter or mid-tier models (like a "Flash" or standard "Pro" variant) before the absolute heaviest architecture goes live. 🌟 Will Advanced/Ultra Users Get it First?Yes, paid subscribers and enterprise users will almost certainly get priority access. Historically, Google reserves its most capable, frontier-level intelligence tiers for its premium tiers. Gemini Advanced (Paid Subscribers): Flagship reasoning capabilities and high-compute models are traditionally locked behind the Google One AI Premium subscription tier. Advanced users will likely be the first general consumers to interact with Gemini 4's full-scale capabilities. Enterprise & API Developers: High-paying Google Cloud customers and developers usually gain early API access via tiered pricing to build and scale applications before a wider consumer rollout takes place. Are you asking because you want to know when the API endpoints will open for building apps, or are you tracking when it will replace the model in the consumer Gemini web app?
