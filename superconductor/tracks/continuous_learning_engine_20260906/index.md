## Summary
Implements the Continuous Learning Engine (CLE) for Superconductor, enabling autonomous agents to learn on the job and synthesize reusable skills over time. Grounded in state-of-the-art research (Voyager, ExpeL, Reflexion, DSPy) conducted via DeerFlow 2.0, this track delivers a complete closed-loop architecture: trajectory harvesting from swarm and quorum executions, reflective invariant mining for remediation failures, workflow distillation into standard `SKILL.md` candidates, a secure staging area in `.agents/skills/incubating/`, an automated vetting gate (syntax, Dogma, canary testing), and the `/superconductor:learn` CLI promotion workflow.
